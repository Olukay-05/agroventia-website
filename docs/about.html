About AgroVentia Inc.

Our Mission

To make agricultural trade more accessible and commercially effective by connecting qualified supply with genuine market demand.

Our Vision

To become a trusted international trading counterparty for agricultural producers, processors and buyers seeking reliable access to new markets and supply opportunities.

Our Story

AgroVentia Inc. is a Canadian agricultural trading company built to connect reliable agricultural supply with genuine international demand. From our base in Canada and our developing sourcing relationships across West Africa, we identify and develop commercially viable trade opportunities between producers, processors and buyers. Our business operates in both directions. We develop West African agricultural and botanical supply for international markets while originating Canadian agricultural commodities for qualified buyers abroad. Transactions may begin with a buyer requirement or with competitive supply identified at origin. Our focus is practical: understand the commercial requirement, identify suitable counterparties, align specifications and terms, and coordinate the transaction through to shipment.
